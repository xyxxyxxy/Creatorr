SponsorBlock

Uses SponsorBlock data from sponsor.ajay.app

Mark adds chapter markers; remove cuts those spans from archive downloads. The same category cannot be in both lists (category guide).

yt-dlp chapters are added by default. SponsorBlock mark chapters are added on top of those.

Cut these categories from downloads.

Encoding

By default, cuts are only spliced at keyframes, so cut points are often early or late. Re-encode will cut at exact timestamps without needing keyframes. Because re-encode is heavy on the host, it is scheduled as a separate low priority system task.

When a section is cut, Creatorr can insert a short info card (~2s) naming the removed category and the duration skipped.
