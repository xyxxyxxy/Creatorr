Ignore videos

Refresh sidecars

Enqueue sidecar refresh (thumb / subs / NFO) for selected videos with packed media. Others are skipped. Never rewrites info.json.

{{if .SeriesID}}{{end}}

Edit metadata

Fields shared by every selected video are pre-filled except Special kind (always No change until you pick a value). Clear a field back to empty for No change. Filled fields replace that value on every selected video. Genres, tags, and actors replace the whole list when you add at least one entry (actor order is kept). Upload date is preserved. Changing Special kind may enqueue a file rename.

{{if .SeriesID}}{{end}}
Special kind
