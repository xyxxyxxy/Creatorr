How to export cookies

Extension: cookies.txt (Firefox). Paste Netscape format only.

  1. Install cookies.txt and enable it in private windows.
  2. Open a new private window, navigate to the video provider, and log in.
  3. Open a gated video once in that session (age gate / “sign in to confirm”) so the account is verified.
  4. Export cookies for the video host and any related login/SSO host (not only “Current container” / current site - those often omit SSO cookies the extractor needs).
  5. Paste the Netscape jar here. Re-export after the browser rotates the session (yt-dlp warns when cookies are no longer valid).
  6. Keep Use FlareSolverr Off on this host when relying on account cookies: Flare and a non-empty jar are mutually exclusive (Flare merges anonymous cookies over the jar and would overwrite session cookies).
  7. Smart cookie usage (On): learn per source when the jar is needed; start without cookies and retry once on failure; when fallbacks are common, prefer cookies first and probe without cookies periodically. Prefer Off for always-auth hosts if you want the jar on every first try.
