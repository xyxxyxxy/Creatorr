Maturity

Right after upload, sites often offer only early low quality, and sidecars (especially community subtitles) may appear much later. Maturity waits a delay after the upload date, then once re-fetches media (download or stream re-pack) and/or NFO/thumb/subs when the video was first acquired before that deadline.
